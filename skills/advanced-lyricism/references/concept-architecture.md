## 1. Composition role

This layer turns the brief into pressure, contradiction, causality, scale, feedback, perspective and a controlled conceptual search space.

- It receives the brief, supplied creative material, context and voice constraints.
- It produces the pressure model, contradiction, governing image, scale movement and arc logic that feed narrative, lexical, rhetorical and hook construction.
- The paired template holds the live model, candidate structures and locked constraints while the lyric develops.

## 2. Core craft model

Concept work establishes the pressure, contradiction and change that line-level craft must serve.

## Pressure before topic
A topic is a noun; a section needs a force. Reduce the section to one sentence:
- I want X, but Y makes wanting it dangerous; X looks like strength until its cost appears.
- Two true descriptions of the same event refuse to reconcile; The action that solves the immediate problem creates the next one.
- The same image means one thing before the turn and another afterward.

Everything else is selected against that pressure.

## Technique budget
Craft dimensions are coupled. Preserve their trade-offs and discard fixed quotas.

Typical trade-offs:
- multisyllabic density vs sensory/narrative room;; internal saturation vs syntactic clarity;
- entendre density vs clean surface meaning;; rapid cadence vs image complexity;
- explanation vs listener inference;; reference density vs the space required to establish each reference.

Choose a section bias—**technical**, **content**, or **balanced**—then create contrast. Do not max every axis.

## Semantic fields before rhyme fields
Build 3–5 fields:
1. physical scene/objects;
2. verbs/procedures;
3. emotional pressure or a collision between defensible values;
4. technical/cultural vocabulary native to the subject;
5. counter-field/opposites.

Only then derive sound families. This keeps rhyme from choosing the subject.

## Domain mining
A durable conceit needs operations, not just nouns. Extract:
- objects and roles;; verbs and procedures;
- rules;; states/transitions;
- failures;; measurements;
- jargon;; visual properties.

Map each item to the subject by a real shared property. If a field yields two strong mappings and six strained ones, use the two; do not force an extended metaphor.

## Collision: two domains -> third meaning
Use collision as a lyric generator:
1. extract exact properties from domain A;
2. choose domain B with structural overlap **and** meaningful contrast;
3. collide objects, verbs, rules, states, or measurements;
4. reject recognition-only name drops;
5. ask what **emergent third property** appears only because the domains met;
6. mine one second-order implication: what else follows if the mapping is true?

Useful contrasts may include mundane/extreme, childhood/adult consequence, luxury/hardship, medicine/emotion, sport/real stakes, bureaucracy/intimacy, geometry/relationship. They are resources, never quotas.

## Inversion: reverse, then interrogate
1. state the obvious assumption;
2. invert it cleanly;
3. resist treating the inversion as automatically clever;
4. ask what constraint or hidden condition would make it true;
5. keep only the consequence/image that survives.

Constraint archaeology belongs inside concept work: status can reveal cost; protection can become confinement; escape can create the trap.

## Contradiction holding
Do not flatten a tension into a slogan. Make both sides concrete:
- pride / shame;; tenderness / threat;
- loyalty / cost;; confidence / vigilance;
- success / dependence;; survivor logic / moral consequence.

Search for a formulation that explains why both remain true. Contradiction is generative when it changes behaviour, image choice, or section direction.

## Opposition, power, and costly choice
Map the agent, visible want, hidden need or fear, and at least one opposing force with its own competent motive. When a person or group absorbs blame or cost for a larger system, make the scapegoat/agent relation explicit. Opposition should test a value, weakness, loyalty, or dependency rather than exist only to prove the speaker right.

Mastery is demonstrated through action under pressure: what the speaker notices, sacrifices, refuses, miscalculates, or cannot control. Power without limit produces no test; success without cost produces no revelation. When several value claims remain defensible, preserve the tragic choice instead of flattening one side into a thesis prop.

## Scale: preserve the invariant while changing dimensions
Scale is a zoom control, not a standalone subsystem.

Change one or more axes:
- **magnitude:** body -> object -> room -> street -> city -> system;; **time:** second -> night -> year -> generation;
- **social dimension:** one person -> pair -> family/crew -> institution;; **abstraction:** sensation -> routine -> rule -> structure.

The useful question is: **what stays the same while scale changes?** If no invariant survives, the move is just a bigger metaphor.

## Feedback loops and delay
Model recurring pressure as:
`condition -> action -> short-term result -> delayed cost/reward -> changed condition`

Distinguish:
- **reinforcing loop:** each cycle intensifies itself;; **balancing loop:** action pushes back toward a limit.

Ask where the delay sits and where a small intervention would alter the loop. Useful domains include retaliation, debt, attention, avoidance, distrust, status, addiction, success pressure, and relationship repair. A repeated hook can change one variable on each return so the listener feels the loop tighten or loosen.

## Sequence, path dependence, irreversibility
Order is structural when A->B cannot be swapped with B->A without changing meaning.

Test:
- what becomes unavailable after this unit?; what earlier detail is reclassified by a later reveal?
- what must happen first for the next action to exist?; is the effect immediate, delayed, or cumulative?
- can the speaker reverse the decision, or only respond to its consequence?; what is the critical path through the section?

Sequence creates states, not merely chronology.

## Meta-pattern / motif
A recurrence earns motif status when it survives across contexts and predicts something:
1. detect repeated property;
2. collect instances from more than one domain/scene;
3. extract the invariant;
4. test a boundary case;
5. use the principle to predict a later return;
6. translate that return into an image, hook, rhyme family, or decision.

Repetition alone is not a meta-pattern.

## Candidate selection and continued development
When options multiply:
1. set a quality threshold from the brief;
2. compare candidates pairwise on specificity, pressure, generative depth, sonic potential, voice fit, and section longevity;
3. reality-test the winner against an actual line/scene;
4. keep a fallback for the main downside;
5. lock the strongest candidate as the baseline;
6. continue every remaining craft layer and generate further variants wherever another tool, domain, scale, viewpoint, cadence, image system, or performance choice can compound or stress-test it.

Use the threshold as a quality floor and comparison aid, not as an early exit from the lyric work.

## Completion check
Before drafting, know:
- pressure;; viewpoint;
- dominant field/domain;; scale;
- first irreversible change;; likely consequence;
- motif return;; technique bias;
- what you are deliberately leaving simple.

## Computational concept search

Concept work benefits from **controlled search**, not unbounded brainstorming.

### Weighted structural grammar
Generate relationships such as:
- setting -> pressure -> choice -> consequence -> residue;; claim -> concrete support -> resistant detail -> qualified claim;
- ordinary object -> exact property -> human pressure -> transformed meaning;; hook invariant -> changed condition -> altered return.

Use `idea_lab.py --mode grammar` to produce scaffolds. Fill them with the user's material, not stock imagery.

### Mutation dimensions
When a concept is almost working, mutate one dimension:
- scale;; timeframe;
- viewpoint;; syntax delay;
- payoff position;; image distance;
- contradiction;; register;
- silence;; consequence;
- callback function;; sonic frame;
- delivery.

This preserves the strong core while searching locally.

### Collision
A useful collision is not “put two random nouns together.”

For domains A and B:
1. list exact operations/properties in A;
2. list exact operations/properties in B;
3. locate a structural overlap;
4. write a surface statement that works without explanation;
5. identify what third meaning emerges;
6. test the second-order implication.

`idea_lab.py --mode collide` returns the questions, not the answer.

### Curves
Concept sections can be planned as changing parameters:
- pressure;; emotional exposure;
- image distance;; density;
- certainty;; consequence.

`arc_generator.py` can create seeded relative curves. Use them to provoke alternatives, not to automate drama.

### Feedback
After a variant works, lock the successful property and change another. This is how the generative-music idea of iterative parameter control becomes a lyric-writing method.

## 4. Decision method

### 4.1. Frame
- Name the section function, hard constraints, and the complete set of craft objectives and questions; record them in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.2. Baseline
- Describe the present behaviour before judging it; Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.3. Hinge
- Identify the full set of words, bars, events, and performance moments involved in the binding issue.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.4. Mechanism
- Explain how the suspected variable creates the observed effect; Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.5. Isolate
- For causal comparison, hold strong dimensions fixed while varying each suspected lever separately; then combine every improvement that survives its isolated test and record the results in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.6. Material and tool support
- Ground the decision in supplied creative material, direct reading or rehearsal, and every relevant representation, detector or generator.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.7. Audition
- Compare baseline and candidate in the medium that matters; Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.8. Collateral
- Check meaning, voice, rhyme, rhythm, performance, sequence, and consequence as relevant.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.9. Commit
- Keep the most effective change set that survives audition without analytical explanation; Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.

### 4.10. Handoff
- Pass observations, locked decisions, open questions, and generated candidates into every connected domain that can use them.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/concept-architecture.md`.
