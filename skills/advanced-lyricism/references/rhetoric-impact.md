## 1. Composition role

This layer turns pressure and language into impact through entendre, collision, inversion, understatement, property-locked metaphor, callbacks and delayed resolution.

- It receives concept pressure, scene causality, lexical fields, voice limits and placement constraints.
- It produces surface/second readings, collision logic, setup-payoff chains and callbacks for rhyme, prosody, hooks and revision.
- The paired template tests whether each device changes interpretation, pressure or recall rather than merely decorating the line.

## 2. Core craft model

Technique earns its place when it changes interpretation, pressure, or recall.

## Surface-first entendre
For any double/triple reading:
1. literal sentence works;
2. each added reading is supported by the **same actual words/sounds**;
3. every layer is complete enough to paraphrase;
4. the added layer increases pressure, precision, humour, irony, or thematic connection;
5. pronunciation and syntax survive performance.

Do not count literal + vague association + recognisable name as a triple.

## Triangulated meaning
When three domains meet, test each route separately:
- reading A;; reading B;
- reading C.

Then test the intersection: does the third layer change the line, or merely decorate it? This preserves the practitioner “triangulated entendre” method.

## Punchline architecture
A punch often has:
- **setup constraint** — narrows listener expectation;; **pivot** — a word/sound/property that can turn;
- **landing** — forces reclassification.

Useful forms:
- paraprosdokian / late semantic turn;; garden-path reparse;
- expected rhyme withheld or replaced (“mind rhyme”);; callback that changes an earlier line;
- property-based comparison revealed late;; syntactic suspension resolved by the punch.

The landing need not be bar-final if accent, syntax, and silence give it space.

## Collision and re-signification
A reference is not a punchline because the listener recognises it.

Procedure:
1. extract exact property from reference/domain A;
2. map it to a real property in subject/domain B;
3. identify what changes because the two contexts collide;
4. keep the surface sentence coherent without explanation.

Useful collision families:
- mundane / extreme;; innocent / adult consequence;
- consumer object / hard reality;; sport or game rule / real stakes;
- medicine / emotion;; bureaucracy / intimacy;
- geometry / relationship;; domestic routine / public crisis.

Keep **semantic mismatch + exact property mapping** without imposing violence, pop-culture, or technique quotas.

In an explicitly MLE/drill-aware brief, innocent-reference re-signification can use a recognisable property rather than a bare name-drop. Seed routes include Dot Cotton -> smoking/double-barrel wordplay, Peppa Pig -> pig/police association, Postman Pat -> delivery, Bob the Builder -> construction/setup, Mario Kart -> vehicle collision, Michael Phelps -> swimming, Lisa Simpson -> zig-zag silhouette, Tony Stark -> iron/chest imagery, and Cinderella -> broom/cleaning. These are property maps, not reusable punchlines: verify the intended sense, cultural currency and speaker fit, then build a fresh surface sentence. Choose the mapping lane from the actual concept and speaker; hard-content mappings remain available whenever they strengthen that lane.

### UK drill collision palette

When the brief calls for a hard-content drill lane, useful collision systems include:

- mundane domestic or service language against severe consequence;
- consumer brands or electronics against bodily, social or institutional cost;
- children's media, games or school vocabulary against adult stakes;
- geometry, arithmetic or procedural terminology that remains literally exact;
- self-aware admission that implicates the speaker or listener instead of merely escalating bravado.

The collision must preserve two complete readings and the chosen voice. A name-drop without an exact shared property is not a collision, and no palette item is a quota.

## Product / institutional subversion
Brands, products, schools, courts, hospitals, transport, retail, apps, or bureaucratic language can create impact when their **real operational property** maps to the lyric. Recognition is not enough.

Use the institutional term accurately; incorrect jargon breaks the second reading.

## Metaphor-locked rhyme
Choose a domain, extract its native lexicon, then find rhyme anchors inside it. Let the sound architecture prove commitment to the conceit.

Exit when:
- mappings become strained;; jargon needs explanation;
- rhyme starts inventing events;; the domain has already paid off.

## Incongruity and comic pressure
Comic pressure includes:
- mismatch between register and event;; verbal play that exposes social rules;
- mistaken identity/reclassification;; transgression followed by social correction, escape, or changed norm;
- body/material detail puncturing abstraction;; wit as status conflict.

Humour can release pressure, intensify it, or expose contradiction. Do not add a joke merely because the section is dark.

## Deadpan / absurdist logic
State an impossible, disproportionate, or socially strange inference in an ordinary practical register. The effect depends on tonal control: if delivery signals “here is the joke” too loudly, the mismatch weakens.

## Inversion
Establish the default hierarchy, then reverse it for a reason:
- winner becomes dependent;; possession becomes burden;
- protection becomes confinement;; status reveals cost.

A naked reversal is a slogan; attach an image or consequence.

## Contradiction
A strong line can retain two incompatible truths rather than choose one. Use parallel syntax, zeugma, paired images, or a callback whose second appearance reverses the first.

## Scale turn
Move micro<->macro at the impact point while preserving one invariant:
- one gesture -> institution;; one night -> generation;
- body detail -> system;; private routine -> public consequence.

Scale is not “make it bigger”; it is “show the same pressure at another order.”

## Callback
Return an earlier:
- noun;; rhyme family;
- number;; gesture;
- image;; phrase;
- location.

Change at least one of:
- speaker;; stakes;
- tense;; interpretation;
- consequence;; scale.

The return should make the listener revise memory of the first occurrence.

## Understatement and containment
A calm practical sentence around intense material can create irony, dread, humour, or authority. Select it when that tonal mismatch strengthens the speaker and moment.

## Dramatic irony
Let the audience know something the speaker/character does not. Ordinary words then carry a second pressure without requiring a pun.

This is especially useful when repeating a hook or scene: the same literal line can become darker after the listener learns more.

## Rhetorical devices by function
- **metonymy** — system compressed into associated object;; **synecdoche** — whole/part compression;
- **zeugma** — one verb binds semantically different objects;; **dramatic irony** — audience knowledge diverges from speaker knowledge;
- **foil** — contrast reveals properties;; **refrain** — stable form, changed context;
- **anaphora** — repetition establishes prediction;; **litotes** — understatement;
- **personification** — object receives agency;; **hyperbole** — controlled exaggeration;
- **allusion** — external structure/property imported economically.

Name the device only if it helps construction or diagnosis.

## Reference discipline
Brands, games, films, athletes, places, events, and technical fields earn space when they supply a precise:
- visual feature;; action;
- rule;; status relation;
- mechanism;; fact;
- emotional association;; plot parallel.

Verify obscure/current properties when the bar depends on them. Recognition alone is not meaning.

## Impact ladder
Strengthen a weak clever line one axis at a time:
1. concrete image;
2. exact property mapping;
3. personal consequence;
4. stronger rhythmic placement;
5. less explanation;
6. contrast;
7. callback;
8. scale/viewpoint turn.

Do not stack all eight.

## Restraint
A verse where every line demands a re-listen can fail in real time. Plain connective language, one unlayered image, or a breath can make the next dense line legible.

The advanced move is often **distribution**, not maximal density.

## Technique opportunity rather than quota

Do not prescribe “two entendres per four bars” or “one pop-culture reference per verse.”

Instead ask where the material already contains a hinge:
- ambiguous pivot word;; exact cross-domain property;
- withheld complement;; repeated image;
- institutional term with ordinary meaning;; object whose operation mirrors the human problem;
- phrase that can change referent on return.

Then select the technique that exploits the hinge.

### Surface-first test
For any layered line:
- read only the surface;; paraphrase it;
- remove the reference knowledge;; check whether syntax and image still work.

If the line collapses without annotation, the hidden reading is carrying too much structural weight.

### Property lock
For metaphor/analogy:
1. name exact source-domain property;
2. name exact target-domain property;
3. make the wording true in both domains;
4. remove any word that exists only to signal cleverness.

### Delayed impact
A punch can be amplified by:
- syntax suspension;; rhyme delay;
- silence;; line break;
- callback after consequence;; semantic reveal one bar later.

Impact is not synonymous with line-final rhyme.

### 4.1. Frame
- Name the section function, hard constraints, and the complete set of craft objectives and questions; record them in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.2. Baseline
- Describe the present behaviour before judging it; Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.3. Hinge
- Identify the full set of words, bars, events, and performance moments involved in the binding issue.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.4. Mechanism
- Explain how the suspected variable creates the observed effect; Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.5. Isolate
- For causal comparison, hold strong dimensions fixed while varying each suspected lever separately; then combine every improvement that survives its isolated test and record the results in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.6. Material and tool support
- Ground the decision in supplied creative material, direct reading or rehearsal, and every relevant representation, detector or generator.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.7. Audition
- Compare baseline and candidate in the medium that matters; Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.8. Collateral
- Check meaning, voice, rhyme, rhythm, performance, sequence, and consequence as relevant.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.9. Commit
- Keep the most effective change set that survives audition without analytical explanation; Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.

### 4.10. Handoff
- Pass observations, locked decisions, open questions, and generated candidates into every connected domain that can use them.
- Record the decision in `assets/templates/rhetoric-impact.md`.
