## Quick operating card

Revision maps every relevant weakness and opportunity, their interactions and priorities, then coordinates repairs across all owning layers.

## Eight passes
1. **Pressure / meaning** — can each line be paraphrased; does it serve the section?
2. **Causality / agency / consequence** — who acts; what changes; what remains afterward?
3. **Lexical system** — concrete words, accurate domain terms, coherent register, motif logic.
4. **Rhyme / phonology** — stressed sound, family quality, internal/end placement, predictability.
5. **Flow / bar line** — entry, accents, density, breath, closure/enjambment, gear changes.
6. **Voice** — viewpoint, syntax, pronunciation, local register, humour, emotional distance.
7. **Freshness / impact** — stock metaphors, obvious rhyme pairs, empty name-drops, uniform image distance.
8. **Performance** — repeated stumbles, emergency breaths, unintended stress, swallowed key words.

Complete every applicable pass. Use the findings to set depth and order: repair upstream causes first, then rerun and deepen every downstream pass affected by those changes.

## Out-loud protocol
The original revision system’s strongest invariant survives unchanged in spirit:
1. perform at intended tempo;
2. perform again without looking at the page where possible;
3. mark every repeated stumble;
4. mark involuntary breaths;
5. clap/tap the pulse and note where natural stress fights it;
6. circle words that disappear or blur;
7. revise every repeatable performance failure while retaining one-off observations for comparison;
8. retest.

A one-off stumble may be performance noise. A repeatable stumble is a performance signal.

## Meaning test
For each clever line:
- what does the surface sentence mean?
- what does each added reading mean?
- which word supports each reading?
- is any layer merely an association or name-drop?
- would the line still work if the listener misses the hidden layer?

If the surface collapses, the wordplay has become annotation-dependent.

## Predictability test
Predictability can enter through:
- repeated orthographic suffix;
- same bar ending shape;
- same rhyme position;
- same sentence length;
- same image distance;
- same emotional volume;
- same reference type.

Do not ban repetition. Ask whether repetition creates pattern, motif, hook identity, or merely exposes the next move.

## Filler test
Delete or rewrite a line if its main function is:
- completing a rhyme family after meaning is exhausted;
- restating the previous line without development;
- supplying generic scene language;
- maintaining genre costume;
- explaining a reference the line failed to make legible.

A connective line can be plain and still necessary if it changes time, place, agency, or breath.

## Anti-pattern library: lyric translations of visual-narrative failures

### False consequence / aesthetic consequence
Large event, no material/interpersonal/psychological trace. Fix aftermath, not adjective volume.

### Sagging middle
Activity repeats without forcing a new plan, understanding, or cost. Escalate consequence or change strategy.

### Formulaic misapplication
A useful grid, four-part arc, rhyme quota, or “cinematic” rule becomes a template obeyed after its function has disappeared. Restore function; keep only the scaffolding that helps.

### Scope inflation
Too many domains, references, characters, time periods, or ideas compete for one section. Reduce to the pressure-bearing set.

### Reactive speaker
The voice receives plot but reveals no choice, interpretation, or changed stance. Give agency or purposeful witness.

### False arc
The lyric announces growth/damage/loyalty without showing changed behaviour, language, relation, or choice.

### Thesis-only opponent
An “other” exists only to prove the speaker right. Give the opposing position a concrete desire, competence, or cost.

### Expository / on-the-nose speech
Dialogue tells both parties what they already know or labels the emotion instead of acting through it.

### Therapy-transcript drift
Introspection becomes clinically explicit but dramatically inert. Add situation, addressee, resistance, or consequence.
